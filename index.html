Sing to Staff

Sing or hum a short tune. You get the notes on a staff and as guitar tab, with key and tempo guessed for you.

Tap to sing
Up to 30 seconds. One note at a time works best.
EXAMPLE A synthesized hum of “Ode to Joy”, sung slightly flat. Record your own to replace it.
